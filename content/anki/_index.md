# Anki decks

Anki is the canonical spaced-repetition system for language study. The
site's own progress trackers (quiz scores, drill history, course
checkpoints) exist to guide the course flow in the browser; they are not a
substitute for a real SRS. These decks let you carry the same items into
Anki and keep them for years, offline, under your own collection.

## Download

- **[vocab.apkg](/downloads/anki/vocab.apkg)** — 300 vocabulary cards (about 200 KB)
- **[drill.apkg](/downloads/anki/drill.apkg)** — 2,589 cloze cards (about 3.4 MB)

In Anki: File → Import, choose the file, keep the default deck
destination. The decks arrive under a `Coptic Reference::` parent so they
do not mix with collections you already keep.

## What is in each deck

**Vocabulary (300 cards).** Every English↔Coptic quiz item, both directions,
all three difficulties. The front is the prompt; the back is the correct
choice plus the dictionary's own gloss of the answer's entry.

**Cloze drills (2,589 cards).** One card per drilled verse from the six
parsed corpus works. The front is the verse with its content word blanked;
the back is the missing form, its lemma, and a link to the dictionary entry
on this site.

Both decks are generated at build time from the same banks the browser
serves — one source of truth, two presentations. Nothing is typed by hand
and nothing is invented.
