# Pronunciation practice

Pick one of the one hundred most frequent lemmas, record yourself saying it,
and listen back against the transliteration. This is a self-comparison
tool, not a pronunciation score. Coptic has no validated ASR. Listen to
yourself against the transliteration and use your ear.

Your recording stays in this page's memory. It is never uploaded, never
saved, and discarded when you navigate away.
