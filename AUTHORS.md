## Upstream data sources

**The authors of the data are not the authors of the site.** This project
compiles and presents other people's work. Under CC BY-SA 4.0 that means the
originators below are credited on **every entry**, in the per-entry `Sources`
panel, and prominently on [/licences/](licences.md) — which is generated from
`LICENSES.csv` and is the authoritative, complete, machine-checked list.

If you are one of the people below and something is misattributed, that is a bug
and we want to hear about it immediately: see [FAQ.md](FAQ.md) or the suggestion
form on any page.
